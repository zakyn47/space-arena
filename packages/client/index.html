FMining laser on the asteroid in front of you (hold)

Touch

  • Drag the left stick in the direction you want to fly; it always thrusts outside the center. Release to coast.
  • Hold Guns, Shield, Rockets / Mines, Mining or Reverse with your right thumb.
  • Tap Dock near a station. Tap Landscape for fullscreen and a wider view.

Flight is inertial: your ship keeps drifting until you brake. While thrusting, it grips and follows its nose.

PILOT BRIEFING / 03

Rules of the arena

  • One shared world. Every pilot flies in the same persistent space. Fly, mine, fight and upgrade; there is no round end.
  • Credits. Bounties and ore give carried credits. Dock at a station to sell ore and bank them. Banked credits are safe.
  • Death. You lose installed upgrades, carried ore, unbanked credits and all rockets or mines, then respawn at a station in a starter Scout. Banked credits and your captain stay.
  • Safe zones. The green circles around stations disable weapons and shields. Bullets vanish inside them.
  • Docking. Fly within 200 px of a station, slow down and avoid damage for 5 s, then press E. Docking repairs your hull and opens the hangar.
  • Shields block all damage while held, but you cannot shoot. They drain energy and lock for 1 s when empty.
  • Ammunition. Carry either homing rockets (30 damage) or space mines (60 damage to every enemy within 100 px). Buying the other type sells your current ammo back at 50%.
  • Pirates patrol open space and get tougher the farther you fly from the stations. Destroyed pirates drop crates with credits, ore and sometimes gear.
  • Mining. Drill asteroids with the mining laser or shoot them and collect the ore, up to your cargo capacity.
  • Hangar. Buy hulls and Mk1–Mk3 gear. Replaced or sold gear refunds 50%.
  • Captain. Earn XP from banked ore and kills. Each level gives a skill point for gunnery, engineering, piloting or mining. Skills are permanent.
